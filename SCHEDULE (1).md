# Schedule System

## Rolling Schedule

Green Pepper Season 1 operates on a **Rolling Schedule**.

The times shown below are **target start times**.

They are not guaranteed fixed start times.

A match may begin earlier or later depending on:

- Previous match duration
- Overtime
- Technical pauses
- Player disconnects
- Server issues
- Protests
- Administrative decisions

Teams must remain available during their assigned competition window.

### Group Stage BO1

- Target match window: approximately **45–60 minutes**

- Exact timing depends on match progression.

### Playoff BO3

- Target series window: approximately **2 hours 15 minutes**
- Target transition period: **30 minutes**
- Overtime, pauses and technical problems may extend the series.

# GROUP STAGE TIEBREAKERS

If two or more teams finish level on points, the following order applies:

1. **Match Record**
2. **Head-to-Head Result**
3. **Round Difference**
4. **Total Rounds Won**
5. **Strength of Schedule**
6. **Additional Tiebreak Match**, if required

Round Difference is calculated as:

`Rounds Won − Rounds Lost`

For three-way or larger ties, the criteria are applied to the tied teams as a group. Once a position is resolved, the remaining tied teams may be re-evaluated.

Special attention applies to the **4th-place position**, because it determines Playoff qualification.

# SCHEDULE RULES

- All times are listed in **GMT / UTC+0**.
- Published times are **target times**.
- The tournament operates on a **Rolling Schedule**.
- Matches may begin earlier if all required teams are ready and administration approves.
- Matches may begin later due to previous match duration, overtime, pauses, disconnects, technical problems or administrative issues.
- Teams must be ready at least **30 minutes before their target match time**.
- A **15-minute match grace period** applies once a team is officially called to the server.
- Failure to be ready after the grace period may result in a forfeit.
- Teams must remain available according to the rolling schedule.
- Teams may not assume that a target time is a guaranteed fixed start time.
- Administration may adjust the schedule when necessary.
- The final authority on schedule changes is the Tournament Administration.
- Players should not be required to remain in active competition until midnight by design; schedule changes caused by exceptional match conditions remain subject to administration.

---

# PLAYER-FRIENDLY SCHEDULING

Green Pepper Season 1 is designed with player availability in mind.

The schedule intentionally:

- Uses a weekend-based Group Stage.
- Provides a multi-day break before Playoffs.
- Uses target rather than artificially rigid start times.
- Provides recovery time between Playoff series.
- Avoids intentionally scheduling fixed midnight matches.

Teams are nevertheless responsible for being available during their assigned competition windows.
