## 2. Open Registration and VRS Status

Green Pepper Season 1 uses **open registration**. VRS standings are used for seeding, eligibility assessment and direct qualification to the Playoffs; VRS ranking does not constitute an invitation to participate.

- Teams do **not** need existing VRS points merely to register.
- Registration is open to eligible teams competing in the European region, subject to the Rulebook and applicable platform and tournament requirements.
- The tournament is limited to 24 accepted teams.
- Registration is not complete until all required registration details and payment have been confirmed.
- The entry fee is €250 per team.
- The eight highest-ranked eligible teams among accepted registered entrants receive direct places in the Playoffs based on the fixed VRS snapshot described below.
- The remaining 16 teams compete in the Swiss Stage for the eight remaining Playoff places.

VRS eligibility and ranking do not remove a team's obligation to complete registration, meet roster requirements, pay the entry fee, and comply with the Rulebook.

## 3. VRS Snapshot and Direct Playoff Qualification

The tournament uses the official VRS ranking snapshot dated **2 November 2026**.

Eight teams receive direct qualification to the 16-team Playoffs:

1. Eligible teams are assessed using the official VRS snapshot dated 2 November 2026.
2. Direct places are awarded to the eight highest-ranked eligible teams among accepted registered entrants.
3. Teams must be registered, accepted and eligible under the tournament rules.
4. If a direct-qualifying team is not registered or is ruled ineligible, its place passes to the next-highest-ranked eligible registered team.
5. Green Pepper will announce the direct qualifiers after registration and eligibility checks, before the Swiss Stage begins.
6. Changes in VRS rankings after the snapshot date do not change the fixed snapshot used for the tournament's initial VRS seeding and qualification decisions.

Direct Playoff qualification is a seeding and competition-format outcome within an open-registration event. It is **not** a direct invitation to enter the tournament.

## 4. VRS Matching and Roster Continuity

Roster eligibility and continuity are governed by the full provisions in [`RULEBOOK.md`](RULEBOOK.md), particularly Section 1.3.

### VRS-matched teams

- A team is considered VRS-matched when at least three of its five registered starting players match the same official five-player VRS roster in the applicable 2 November 2026 snapshot.
- Substitutes are not counted for the initial VRS-matching decision.
- A VRS-matched team must field at least three players from that matched VRS five-player roster throughout every match, including Playoff matches.
- The team must maintain at least three players from the matched VRS roster on its official tournament roster throughout the event. Falling below the required number may result in disqualification.

### Teams without a VRS match

- A team that is not matched to a VRS roster must field at least three of its five original registered starters throughout every match, including the Playoffs.
- Later roster additions do not change the original five-player reference roster.

### Other roster restrictions

- No two teams may share three or more players from their applicable five-player reference rosters.
- Roster changes and substitutions require approval where specified in the Rulebook.
- Approved roster changes do not remove the applicable three-of-five continuity requirement.
- During the Playoffs, up to two substitute-slot roster changes in total may be permitted, subject to the Rulebook and administrative approval.

The Rulebook is the controlling source for the detailed roster, substitute, and eligibility requirements.

## 5. Seeding Where a Team Has No VRS Ranking

The tournament's seeding procedures are described in the Rulebook. Where a team has no comparable VRS ranking, the documented alternative criteria are applied as follows:

1. The official VRS snapshot dated **2 November 2026** is considered first for eligible VRS-matched teams.
2. If a team has no VRS ranking, the HLTV ranking from the same date may be used where applicable.
3. If the team has neither a VRS nor an HLTV ranking, the average FACEIT ELO of its registered players at the end of 2 November 2026 may be used.
4. A player without a current FACEIT ELO uses their last known ELO.
5. A player who has never had a FACEIT ELO is assigned a value of **1000** for this calculation.

The tournament administration will apply the documented seeding process and publish the relevant seeds and match pairings. The detailed provisions in the Rulebook take precedence if a summary is incomplete.

## 6. Tournament Format

### Direct Playoff Qualifiers

- **8 teams**
- Selected from the highest-ranked eligible registered entrants in the 2 November 2026 VRS snapshot.
- Advance directly to the 16-team Playoff bracket.

### Swiss Stage

- **16 teams** — the eligible registered teams that do not receive a direct Playoff place.
- Match format: **Best-of-Three (BO3)**.
- A team qualifies for the Playoffs after **3 match wins**.
- A team is eliminated after **3 match losses**.
- **8 teams** advance from the Swiss Stage.
- Teams are paired against opponents with the same or a similar match record.
- Teams will not face the same opponent more than once.
- Swiss pairings, seeds and tiebreak procedures are subject to the official Schedule and Rulebook.

### Playoffs

The Playoffs consist of 16 teams:

- 8 VRS direct qualifiers.
- 8 Swiss Stage qualifiers.
- Single-Elimination bracket.
- Round of 16, Quarter-finals and Semi-finals: **BO3**.
- Grand Final: **BO5**, scheduled for **13 December 2026 at 16:00 CET**, unless an official announcement changes the schedule.

A team is eliminated after one Playoff match loss.

## 8. Competitive Platform and Integrity

- All official matches are played through the designated **FACEIT** tournament infrastructure.
- **FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory** for every player in official matches.
- Players must use their own registered Steam and FACEIT accounts.
- Account sharing, unauthorized stand-ins, cheating, prohibited software, match-fixing and attempts to bypass integrity checks are prohibited.
- Teams must comply with the Rulebook's match, roster, technical, conduct, protest and disciplinary provisions.
- Green Pepper administration may verify player and roster eligibility and investigate reported integrity concerns in accordance with the Rulebook.
