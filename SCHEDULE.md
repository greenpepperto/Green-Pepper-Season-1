## Group Stage

- **Format:** 6 groups of 4 teams.
- **System:** Single round robin; each team plays the other teams in its group once.
- **Match format:** Best-of-3 (BO3).
- **Round windows:**
  - Round 1: 2–5 November
  - Round 2: 6–9 November
  - Round 3: 10–13 November
  - Make-up window: 14–15 November
- **Qualification:** The top two teams from each group, plus the four highest-ranked third-place teams, advance to the 16-team Playoffs.
- Teams may reschedule a group-stage match only within its published round window, with mutual agreement and Admin approval where required by the Rulebook.

## Playoffs

- **Format:** 16-team double-elimination bracket.
- **Match format:** BO3, with the Grand Final BO5 unless the organizers announce otherwise before the Playoffs begin.
- **Proposed round windows:**

| Date | Playoff milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 20–23 November | Opening round |
| 24–27 November | Upper-bracket quarter-finals and lower-bracket opening matches |
| 28 November – 1 December | Upper-bracket semi-finals and lower-bracket matches |
| 2–5 December | Upper-bracket final and continuing lower-bracket matches |
| 6–9 December | Lower-bracket progression |
| 10–11 December | Lower-bracket final / final preparations |
| 13 December | Grand Final — BO5 |

The exact match pairings and daily bracket schedule will be published once the group-stage standings are confirmed. Playoff rescheduling requires Admin approval and must preserve bracket integrity.

## Match Scheduling Guidelines

- **Platform:** FACEIT. FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory for all players.
- **Default match time:** 20:00 Central European local time (CET), unless the official match schedule states otherwise.
- **Readiness:** Teams should be ready in their matchroom at the scheduled time. The Rulebook's 15-minute grace period applies unless match-specific instructions state otherwise.
- **Results:** Captains must ensure match results are recorded correctly and report any issue through the official support channel.
- **Reschedules:** A match is not considered rescheduled until the change is confirmed by the opponent and approved by an Admin where required.
