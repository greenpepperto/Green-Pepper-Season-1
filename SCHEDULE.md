## VRS Direct Qualification

- **Eight teams** qualify directly for the Playoffs based on the official Valve Regional Standings (VRS).
- Direct places go to the eight highest-ranked eligible teams among the accepted tournament entrants, using the official VRS ranking available at the registration deadline.
- Teams must be registered, accepted and eligible to receive a direct playoff place.
- If a direct-qualifying team is not registered or is ruled ineligible, the place passes to the next-highest-ranked eligible registered team.
- The VRS snapshot date and list of direct qualifiers will be published after registration closes.

## Swiss Stage

- **Participants:** 16 teams that do not receive a direct VRS playoff place.
- **Format:** 16-team Swiss Stage.
- **Match format:** Best-of-3 (BO3).
- **Qualification:** A team qualifies for the Playoffs after **three match wins**.
- **Elimination:** A team is eliminated after **three match losses**.
- **Advancement:** The first eight teams to meet the qualification condition advance to the Playoffs.
- Teams are paired against opponents with the same or a similar match record. Teams will not face the same opponent more than once.
- Swiss pairings, seeding and tiebreak procedures will be published before the first round. The organizers may adjust round windows to ensure all qualification matches are completed fairly.
- Teams may reschedule a Swiss match only within its published round window, with mutual agreement and Admin approval where required by the Rulebook.

## Playoffs

- **Format:** 16-team **Single-Elimination** bracket.
- **Participants:** Eight VRS direct qualifiers plus eight Swiss Stage qualifiers.
- **Match format:** BO3.
- **Grand Final:** BO5, unless the organizers announce otherwise before the Playoffs begin.
- **Elimination:** One playoff match loss eliminates a team from the tournament.
- **Proposed round windows:**

| Date | Playoff milestone |
| --- | --- |
| 20–25 November | Round of 16 |
| 26 November – 2 December | Quarter-finals |
| 3–9 December | Semi-finals |
| 13 December | Grand Final — BO5 |

The exact pairings and daily bracket schedule will be published once the Swiss Stage is complete. The bracket will be seeded according to the published VRS and Swiss seeding plan. Playoff rescheduling requires Admin approval and must preserve bracket integrity.

## Match Scheduling Guidelines

- **Platform:** FACEIT. FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory for all players.
- **Default match time:** 20:00 Central European local time, unless the official match schedule states otherwise.
- **Readiness:** Teams should be ready in their matchroom at the scheduled time. The Rulebook's 15-minute grace period applies unless match-specific instructions state otherwise.
- **Results:** Captains must ensure match results are recorded correctly and report any issue through the official support channel.
- **Reschedules:** A match is not considered rescheduled until the change is confirmed by the opponent and approved by an Admin where required.
