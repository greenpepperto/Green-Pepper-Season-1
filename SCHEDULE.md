## Tournament Format

### VRS Direct Qualification

- Eight teams qualify directly for the Playoffs based on the official Valve Regional Standings (VRS).
- Direct places go to the eight highest-ranked eligible teams among accepted tournament entrants, using the official VRS ranking snapshot taken on **2 November 2026**.
- Teams must be registered, accepted and eligible by the registration deadline of **15 November 2026**.
- If a direct-qualifying team is not registered or is ruled ineligible, the place passes to the next-highest-ranked eligible registered team.
- The list of direct qualifiers will be published after registration closes and eligibility checks are complete.

### Swiss Stage

- **Participants:** 16 teams that do not receive a direct VRS playoff place.
- **Format:** 16-team Swiss Stage.
- **Match format:** Best-of-3 (BO3).
- **Qualification:** A team advances to the Playoffs after three match wins.
- **Elimination:** A team is eliminated after three match losses.
- **Advancement:** The first eight teams to meet the qualification condition advance to the Playoffs.
- Teams are paired against opponents with the same or a similar match record. Teams will not face the same opponent more than once.
- Swiss pairings, seeding and tiebreak procedures will be published before the first round.

### Playoffs

- **Participants:** 16 teams — eight VRS direct qualifiers and eight Swiss Stage qualifiers.
- **Format:** Single-Elimination bracket.
- **Round of 16, Quarter-finals and Semi-finals:** BO3.
- **Grand Final:** BO5, unless the organizers announce otherwise before the Playoffs begin.
- One playoff match loss eliminates a team from the tournament.
- Exact pairings and the daily bracket schedule will be published once the Swiss Stage is complete.

## Online Match Scheduling Guidelines

### Match Times and Availability

- Scheduled match starts may be assigned between **11:00 and 20:30 CET** during the Swiss Stage and scheduled Playoff match days, unless a specific time is published for the match.
- The **Grand Final is scheduled for 13 December 2026 at 16:00 CET**.
- The published match schedule will identify each match's exact start time and any deadline for completing the series.
- Teams must be ready in their FACEIT matchroom at the scheduled time, with all eligible players available and FACEIT Anti-Cheat running.
- Teams should remain reachable through the official tournament communication channel throughout their assigned match window.
- There is no venue check-in and no requirement to arrive 90 minutes before a match.
- Matches are scheduled online; teams are responsible for having a stable internet connection, a working PC, required game updates and access to FACEIT.

### Readiness and Grace Period

- The Rulebook's **15-minute grace period** applies unless match-specific instructions state otherwise.
- If a team is not ready after the grace period, the team may be subject to a forfeit or other sanction under the Rulebook.
- Players must use the registered and approved roster. Roster eligibility and substitutions are governed by the Rulebook.

### Platform and Anti-Cheat

- All matches must be played on **FACEIT** using the matchroom and server arrangements designated by the organizers.
- **FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory** for every player participating in a match.
- Players must comply with FACEIT requirements and all tournament rules relating to accounts, game integrity and prohibited software.
- Players must not share accounts or allow another person to play on their behalf.
- Any technical or anti-cheat issue must be reported promptly to a tournament Admin through the official support channel.

### Results, Evidence and Disputes

- Captains must verify that the final result is recorded correctly on FACEIT.
- Any score discrepancy, server issue, suspected rule violation or dispute must be reported to tournament administration promptly.
- Teams should retain relevant evidence where appropriate, such as screenshots, matchroom details or error messages.
- Results and rulings are subject to verification by tournament administration.

### Rescheduling and Delays

- A match is not considered rescheduled until the opponent has confirmed the change and an Admin has approved it where required.
- Swiss matches may be rescheduled only within the published round window, with mutual agreement and Admin approval where required by the Rulebook.
- Playoff rescheduling requires Admin approval and must preserve bracket integrity.
- Teams must not independently delay a match beyond its assigned window without authorization.
- If a technical problem affects a match, teams must pause further action only as directed by the match Admin and follow the Rulebook's technical-issue procedure.
